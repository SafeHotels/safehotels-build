🍪 Cookie Policy

How we use cookies, and how to change your choice

📅 Last updated: 27 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Some are needed for a site to work; others help the owner understand how it's used.

Cookies we use

  • Your cookie choice: we remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics, using your browser's local storage. This is needed to respect your choice.
  • Google Analytics (only if you accept): counts visits and shows which pages are used, so we can improve the site. These cookies (such as _ga) last up to two years. If you reject, Google Analytics doesn't load at all.

Partner cookies after you click

When you click "Check prices", you go to Stay22, which sends you on to booking sites such as Booking.com or Expedia. Those sites set their own cookies to track bookings so we can be paid a commission. Their cookie and privacy policies apply on their sites. We don't set these cookies on SafeHotels.ai.

Changing your choice

Change your cookie choice. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Questions

Email privacy@safehotels.ai. See also our privacy policy.
